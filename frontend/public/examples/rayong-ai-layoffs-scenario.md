# Scenario — Rayong Factory AI Automation & Mass Layoffs

## Background
The Eastern Seaboard (Rayong/Chonburi) is Thailand's industrial heartland, hosting thousands of
electronics and automotive factories. A major electronics manufacturer announces a "smart
factory" transformation: AI-driven automation and robotics will replace most assembly-line jobs
at its Rayong plant over the next 18 months.

## The plan
- **~4,000 of 6,000 line jobs** to be eliminated; the company offers a "reskilling allowance"
  and early retirement for older workers.
- The plant frames it as **necessary to stay competitive** and retain higher-skilled roles.
- The government touts the automation as **industrial upgrading**, granting tax incentives for
  the robot investment.
- A **union** is forming to fight for severance and retraining guarantees.

## The tension
- **Assembly workers** (many with 15+ years, limited education) face displacement with few
  transferable skills.
- **Migrant workers** (Myanmar/Cambodian, ~30% of the line) are not eligible for most severance
  or retraining and fear losing their work permits.
- **Local shops and housing** depend on factory wages; a layoff wave would ripple through town.
- **Young engineers** on the automation team see it as career growth but face colleague
  resentment.
- **Investors** cheer the efficiency; **labor advocates** call it corporate abandonment.

## What to simulate
> Simulate the next 18 months. How do workers, the union, and management negotiate? Who is
> retrained, who is left behind, and how does the local economy absorb the shock?

# Market Entry Decision — Thailand

### 1. David Kim — CEO
Wants to enter Thailand within two quarters to show board-level growth. Aggressive, high risk-tolerance.
Stance: PUSHES FOR ENTRY. Sees the region as the last big growth market and wants first-mover share.

### 2. Lena Weber — CFO
Guards cash and payback. The local setup costs and FX exposure make her nervous.
Stance: OPPOSES SPEED. Wants a partner-led pilot first and a hard budget cap before any commitment.

### 3. Omar Haddad — Head of APAC
Knows the local distributors, regulators, and pricing norms from twelve years in the region.
Stance: CONDITIONAL. Backs entry, but only with a local partner and a staged rollout, not a full launch.

### 4. Anan Chaiyasit — Incumbent market leader
Runs the dominant local player that would be displaced. Reactive and defensive, with deep distribution.
Stance: RESPONDS TO THREAT. Will cut prices and lock in exclusive retail contracts if a foreign entrant lands.

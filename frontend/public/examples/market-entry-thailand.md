# Market Entry — Entering Thailand Against a Dominant Incumbent

A reality seed for MiroFish. Each `###` person becomes a distinct simulation agent.

### 1. David Kim — CEO (Aggressive, high risk-tolerance)
Wants to enter now and undercut on price to grab share fast.

### 2. Lena Weber — CFO (Conservative, cost-anxious)
Wants to wait a year and de-risk the investment before committing.

### 3. Omar Haddad — Head of APAC (Local market expert)
Knows the Thai market and the incumbent better than anyone in HQ.

### 4. The Incumbent — Market leader (Reactive, defensive)
Holds 70% share; will protect its turf aggressively.

## Relationships
- David → Lena: final say on budget.
- Omar → David: reports to David, but knows the market better than he does.
- The Incumbent ↔ (all): competitor, not an ally.

## Context (cultural layer)
Hofstede: high collectivism, high power distance in the local market.
Market: Thailand. Regulatory uncertainty around data-localisation rules.

## The Tension
David wants to launch in 3 months and undercut on price; Lena wants to wait a year.

# Scenario — Bangkok Port Relocation & Khlong Toei Redevelopment

## Background
Khlong Toei district, Bangkok: ~104,000 residents over 12.565 sq km, anchored by Bangkok Port —
the Port Authority of Thailand's container port on the Chao Phraya, moving ~1.26–1.5 million TEU
a year across 8 berths, served by private terminals alongside PAT.

In July 2026 the Transport Minister ordered a study to move **all** Bangkok Port cargo operations
to Laem Chabang (the deep-sea port ~130 km south-east). This would unlock **2,353 rai** of prime
central-Bangkok land for redevelopment.

## The plan
- **500–600 rai** of the 2,353 reserved for new housing for **12,600+ households (~40,000 people)**
  in the port's informal settlement.
- The remainder slated for a **casino-free entertainment complex, a cruise terminal, a yacht
  marina, and premium residential**.

## The Port Authority's relocation offer (three options)
1. A **33 sqm on-site apartment** (Soi Trimit).
2. **80 sqm of land in Nong Chok** (50 km away).
3. **Cash compensation.**

## The tension
- Residents have heard this before: relocation has been attempted **more than ten times since
  2002** and never succeeded.
- The **33 sqm unit cannot house the two-generation and 5–18-person households** common in the
  settlement.
- The community is **split between those willing to move and those who will fight**.
- **Migrant workers** (~6–7% of residents) are **not counted** in the 12,600-household register
  and would be left with nothing.
- A new **vending mandate reserves market stalls for "poor Thais"**, excluding migrant vendors.
- A 2026 parliamentary remark calling Khlong Toei a place to "easily buy drugs" triggered
  community outrage — dignity/stigma is a live sensitivity.

## What to simulate
> Simulate the next 12 months as the relocation and 2,353-rai redevelopment plan is announced.
> Who supports, who resists, who negotiates? How does the community split between those willing
> to move and those who will fight — and where do the 12,600 settlement households end up?

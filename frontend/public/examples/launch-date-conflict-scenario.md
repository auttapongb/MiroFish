# Launch Date Conflict — Scenario

A B2B SaaS company is three weeks from the planned launch of its flagship analytics release. Engineering has uncovered unresolved test failures in the reporting module, while two flagship customers have already been briefed on the launch date, and a competitor is rumoured to announce a similar feature within the month.

Simulate the team's debate over the next few weeks: who pushes to ship on time, who pushes to delay, what compromises or escalations emerge, and what the company ultimately decides.

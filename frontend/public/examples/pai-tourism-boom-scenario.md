# Scenario — Viral Tourism Boom Overwhelms a Quiet Town

## Background
A small valley town in the northern mountains (population ~15,000), known for rice paddies, a
famous walking street, and a relaxed bohemian vibe. A series of viral short-video posts (shot
at sunrise over the rice terraces) turns the town into a must-visit destination almost overnight.

## The surge
- **Visitors triple in six months**; weekend traffic overwhelms the two-lane roads.
- **Property investors** and boutique-hotel chains move in, buying rice paddies and old wooden
  houses at multiples of their former value.
- **Rents spike**; long-term residents, artists, and small traders face eviction.
- **Trash, noise, and water demand** strain the town's infrastructure; a beloved waterfall is
  cordoned off after damage.

## The tension
- **Older residents and temple elders** mourn the loss of quiet and tradition.
- **Young locals** split between running homestays/cafes for the new crowd and resenting the
  outsiders changing their home.
- **Farmers** are tempted to sell ancestral land vs. keep farming.
- **Environmentalists** warn the surge is unsustainable; **local government** is caught between
  development revenue and community backlash.

## What to simulate
> Simulate the next 12 months. Who profits, who is displaced, and who fights back? Does the
> town embrace the boom, regulate it, or fracture over it?

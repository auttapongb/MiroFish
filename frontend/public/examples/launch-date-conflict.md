# Launch Date Conflict — B2B SaaS

### 1. Maya Chen — VP of Product
Driven the new analytics release for nine months. Believes the features are ready and the market window is now, before the competitor announces.
Stance: PUSHES FOR THE ORIGINAL DATE. A delay hands the competitor the launch news cycle.

### 2. Raj Patel — Head of Engineering
Owns the roadmap and QA gates. Has seen the unresolved test failures and flaky integrations first-hand.
Stance: OPPOSES THE DATE. Wants four more weeks of hardening; a broken launch destroys enterprise trust.

### 3. Sofia Rossi — Chief Revenue Officer
Owns launch marketing and the pipeline. Already briefed two flagship customers on the release date.
Stance: PUSHES FOR THE DATE. A slip means re-contracting and refund pressure on pre-committed deals.

### 4. Tom Becker — Senior Engineer
Works the release branch daily. Knows exactly which bugs are cosmetic and which are data-loss risks.
Stance: SPLIT. Agrees 80% is ready, but won't sign off on shipping the reporting module yet.

# Ride-Hailing Ban Debate — Scenario

A city government is considering a ban on ride-hailing platforms, citing safety and congestion. Incumbent taxi and ride-for-hire drivers back the ban; riders, delivery-dependent small businesses, and young workers are caught in the middle; and a local journalist is shaping public opinion.

Simulate the public debate over the next month: who dominates the narrative, how the government responds to pressure from both sides, and whether the ban is enacted, softened, or withdrawn.

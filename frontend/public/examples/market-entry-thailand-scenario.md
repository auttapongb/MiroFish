# Market Entry Decision — Thailand — Scenario

A foreign company must decide whether to enter the Thai market within two quarters. The CEO wants fast entry for board-level growth; the CFO wants a cash-capped, partner-led pilot; the regional head knows the local distributors and regulators; and a dominant local incumbent is ready to defend its share.

Simulate the decision over the next quarter: what entry mode (if any) wins out, how the incumbent responds, and whether the foreign company commits, partners, or walks away.
